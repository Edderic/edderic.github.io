01

DataplorSenior ML Engineer
2024–2026

Geospatial dataProduction ML

Cut the cost of cleaning
global place names.

Automated name cleaning with a production model that reduced inference cost by 98%.

Place records contain names in many languages, often mixed with promotional text and other clutter. The existing generative approach cost $70,000 per run and could introduce words that were never in the source name.

I trained a model to decide which parts of the original name to keep or remove. This made the task more focused and constrained the output to text already in the record.

The business result: substantially lower operating cost for a recurring data-quality task, with a model designed around the actual cleanup problem.

MasqFitResearch & Engineering Lead
2024–present

Independent researchiOS product

Take a respirator-fit idea
all the way to production.

Secured a $50,000 Kanro Fund grant and led the research, machine learning, and product development behind MasqFit.

Finding a respirator that fits can involve considerable trial and error. MasqFit investigates whether facial measurements and fit-test evidence can help people choose better-fitting respirators.

I recruited participants, collected measurements and fit-test outcomes, developed ML algorithms, and built and released an open-source iOS app. My role spanned the research question, the evidence needed to study it, and the application people can use.
